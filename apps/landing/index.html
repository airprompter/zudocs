How support works here

Every support ticket at Zudocs runs through three prompts — triage, reply and escalate. The prompts do not live in this app's code: they are governed in AirPrompter, versioned, checked against golden cases, rolled out gradually, and delivered to every Zudocs host in three regions the moment a change is approved. The desk shows exactly which version answered each ticket and what came back.

That is the point of Zudocs: it is a real deployment of the AirPrompter Agent SDK, in the open, so you can see how a prompt change reaches running software and how the results come back.
